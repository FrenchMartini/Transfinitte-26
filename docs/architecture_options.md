## 2. Common Reference Model ($1.0\text{ AU} \to 2.7\text{ AU}$)

All candidate architectures must impart the exact same change in Earth's heliocentric mechanical orbital energy ($\Delta E$) and orbital angular momentum ($\Delta L$) to move Earth from a circular orbit at $r_1 = 1.0\text{ AU}$ ($1.495978707 \times 10^{11}\text{ m}$) to a circular orbit at $r_2 = 2.7\text{ AU}$ ($4.0391425089 \times 10^{11}\text{ m}$) within $T_{\max} = 10,000\text{ yr}$ ($3.15576 \times 10^{11}\text{ s}$):

| Reference Quantity | Formula | Exact SI Value | Notes / Applicability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Initial Circular Speed** ($v_1$) | $\sqrt{GM_\odot / r_1}$ | $29,784.692\text{ m/s}$ ($29.785\text{ km/s}$) | Exact two-body circular velocity at $1.0\text{ AU}$ |
| **Target Circular Speed** ($v_2$) | $\sqrt{GM_\odot / r_2}$ | $18,126.386\text{ m/s}$ ($18.126\text{ km/s}$) | Exact two-body circular velocity at $2.7\text{ AU}$ |
| **Initial Orbital Energy** ($E_1$) | $-\frac{GM_\odot M_\oplus}{2 r_1}$ | $-2.649053 \times 10^{33}\text{ J}$ | State function (independent of transfer path) |
| **Target Orbital Energy** ($E_2$) | $-\frac{GM_\odot M_\oplus}{2 r_2}$ | $-9.811306 \times 10^{32}\text{ J}$ | State function (independent of transfer path) |
| **Required Energy Change** ($\Delta E$) | $E_2 - E_1$ | **$+1.667922 \times 10^{33}\text{ J}$** | **Universal for all architectures** |
| **Initial Angular Momentum** ($L_1$) | $M_\oplus r_1 v_1$ | $2.661049 \times 10^{40}\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{s}$ | State function |
| **Target Angular Momentum** ($L_2$) | $M_\oplus r_2 v_2$ | $4.372550 \times 10^{40}\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{s}$ | $L_2 / L_1 = \sqrt{2.7} = 1.643168$ |
| **Required Angular Momentum Change** ($\Delta L$) | $L_2 - L_1$ | **$+1.711501 \times 10^{40}\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{s}$** | **Universal for all architectures** |
| **Hohmann Benchmark $\Delta v$** | $\Delta v_1 + \Delta v_2$ | $6,197.6 + 4,799.6 = \mathbf{10,997.2\text{ m/s}}$ | Impulsive two-burn benchmark ($t_H = 1.258\text{ yr}$); **not executable by low-thrust systems** |
| **Continuous Quasi-Circular Spiral $\Delta v$** | $v_1 - v_2$ | **$11,658.306\text{ m/s}$** ($11.658\text{ km/s}$) | Applies to any continuous prograde tangential low-thrust architecture |
| **Required Tangential Impulse** ($J_{\text{spiral}}$) | $M_\oplus \Delta v_{\text{spiral}}$ | **$6.962573 \times 10^{28}\text{ N}\cdot\text{s}$** | Minimum total impulse delivered to Earth |
| **Required Average Force** ($F_{\theta,\text{avg}}$) | $J_{\text{spiral}} / T_{\max}$ | **$2.206306 \times 10^{17}\text{ N}$** | Average tangential thrust over $10,000\text{ yr}$ |
| **Required Average Mechanical Power** ($\bar{P}_{\text{mech}}$) | $\Delta E / T_{\max} = F_{\theta,\text{avg}}\frac{v_1+v_2}{2}$ | **$5.285326 \times 10^{21}\text{ W}$** | Minimum rate of orbital work done *on Earth* ($30,450\times$ all sunlight hitting Earth) |

### Why Hohmann vs. Low-Thrust Must Be Distinguished
- A **Hohmann transfer** ($\Delta v = 10.997\text{ km/s}$ in $1.258\text{ yr}$) requires two instantaneous velocity jumps ($\Delta v_1 = 6.198\text{ km/s}$, $\Delta v_2 = 4.800\text{ km/s}$). Even if spread over a 1-day burn, $\Delta v_1$ would accelerate Earth at $0.072\text{ m/s}^2$, requiring a thrust of $4.3 \times 10^{23}\text{ N}$ and mechanical power of $1.3 \times 10^{28}\text{ W}$ ($33\times$ the entire luminosity of the Sun!), while subjecting Earth's crust and oceans to global megatsunamis and stripping the Moon behind in a $1\text{ AU}$ orbit.
- A **continuous low-thrust tangential spiral** ($\Delta v = 11.658\text{ km/s}$ over $10,000\text{ yr}$) accelerates Earth at only $a_\theta = 3.694 \times 10^{-8}\text{ m/s}^2$ ($2.65 \times 10^6\times$ below the $0.098\text{ m/s}^2$ safety threshold). Because $a_\theta / g_{\odot}(1\text{ AU}) = 6.23 \times 10^{-6} \ll 1$, the orbit remains naturally quasi-circular ($e \sim 1.2 \times 10^{-5}$) at every instant, and the Moon ($a_{\text{Moon}} = 2.70 \times 10^{-3}\text{ m/s}^2 \gg a_\theta$) stays Adiabatically bound to Earth throughout the entire 10,000-year migration.

## 3. Detailed Evaluation of Candidate Architectures (A–F)

### Candidate A — Detached Ceres-Class Gravitational Tug
**Mechanism:** A Ceres-mass body ($M_{\text{tug},0} = 9.4 \times 10^{20}\text{ kg}$) is stationed ahead of Earth along Earth's prograde velocity vector ($\hat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}$). Its gravity pulls Earth forward with $F_{\text{grav}} = G M_\oplus M_{\text{tug}}(t) / d(t)^2$, while relativistic plasma thrusters ($v_e = 0.3c$, $\gamma = 1.04828$) powered by a remote solar-statite / Dyson beam ($P_{\text{beam}} \approx 1.02 \times 10^{25}\text{ W}$) hold the tug on station.

1. **Governing Equations & Trajectory Law:**
   - Because expelling $M_{\text{prop}} \approx 7.50 \times 10^{20}\text{ kg}$ reduces $M_{\text{tug}}(t)$ from $9.4 \times 10^{20}\text{ kg}$ to $1.90 \times 10^{20}\text{ kg}$ (or $1.40 \times 10^{20}\text{ kg}$ at the full $8.0 \times 10^{20}\text{ kg}$ budget), keeping $d = 1.3 \times 10^9\text{ m}$ fixed causes $F_{\text{grav}}$ to drop by $85\%$ and leaves Earth at $1.65\text{ AU}$.
   - **Variable-Separation Control Law:** Shrinking $d(t) = \sqrt{G M_\oplus M_{\text{tug}}(t) / F_{\text{req}}}$ from $d_0 = 1.303 \times 10^9\text{ m}$ ($3.39\,R_{\oplus\text{Moon}}$) down to $d_f = 5.017 \times 10^8\text{ m}$ ($1.305\,R_{\oplus\text{Moon}}$) maintains constant prograde thrust $F_{\text{req}} = 2.2067 \times 10^{17}\text{ N}$.
2. **Plume Clearance & Relativistic Mass Budget:**
   - Canted at $\phi = 10^\circ$ off the $-\hat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}$ axis, the exhaust beams clear Earth by $13.9\text{–}36.0\,R_\oplus$ (missing Earth and its magnetosphere completely).
   - Required reaction mass:
     $$M_{\text{prop}} = \frac{(M_\oplus + M_{\text{tug},0})\,\Delta v_{\text{spiral}}}{\gamma v_e \cos 10^\circ} = \mathbf{7.500 \times 10^{20}\text{ kg}} \le 8.0 \times 10^{20}\text{ kg}\quad (\text{PASS})$$
   - Required relativistic kinetic jet power:
     $$P_{\text{jet}} = (\gamma - 1)\dot{m}_0 c^2 = \mathbf{1.031 \times 10^{25}\text{ W}} \quad (2.69\%\text{ of } L_\odot)$$
3. **Safety, Waste Heat, and Habitability Risks:**
   - **Tides & Moon:** Surface tide on Earth is $\le 9.4 \times 10^{-10}\text{ m/s}^2$ ($0.086\%$ of lunar tide). Tug perturbation on the Moon at conjunction is $\le 0.025\%$ of Earth's gravity.
   - **Waste Heat:** Even $1\%$ conversion loss ($10^{23}\text{ W}$) radiated isotropically at $d_0$ produces $4,709\text{ W/m}^2$ on Earth's sub-tug hemisphere; therefore, tug radiators **must be directionally shielded away from Earth**.
   - **Habitability Gap at $2.7\text{ AU}$:** Candidate A alone moves Earth to $2.7\text{ AU}$ where solar insolation drops by $2.7^2 = 7.29\times$ to $186.7\text{ W/m}^2$ ($T_{\text{eq}} \approx 157\text{ K}$), freezing Earth solid unless paired with an insolation concentrator (see Candidate F).

### Candidate B — Direct Planetary-Ring / Asteroid Reaction-Mass Propulsion
**Mechanism:** Mass drivers or fusion/ion thrusters mounted directly on an equatorial structural ring around Earth (or fed by imported asteroid/lunar material) expel reaction mass at exhaust velocity $v_e$ to push Earth directly.

1. **Trade-off Between Exhaust Velocity $v_e$, Mass Loss $\Delta m$, and Power $P_{\text{jet}}$:**
   $$\Delta m = M_\oplus \left(1 - e^{-\Delta v_{\text{spiral}} / (\gamma v_e)}\right), \qquad P_{\text{jet}} \approx \frac{1}{2}\dot{m} v_e^2 = \frac{1}{2} F_{\text{req}} v_e$$
   - **$v_e = 30\text{ km/s}$ (electric/mass driver):** $\Delta m = 1.923 \times 10^{24}\text{ kg} = \mathbf{32.2\%\text{ of }M_\oplus}$ ($2,046\times$ Ceres's mass!). Violates `max_mass_loss_fraction: 0.01` by $32\times$.
   - **$v_e = 1,000\text{ km/s}$:** $\Delta m = 6.92 \times 10^{22}\text{ kg} = 1.16\%\,M_\oplus$ ($\approx 1\text{ Moon}$), $P_{\text{jet}} = 1.10 \times 10^{23}\text{ W}$.
   - **$v_e = 3,000\text{ km/s}$ (fusion plasma):** $\Delta m = 2.316 \times 10^{22}\text{ kg} = \mathbf{0.388\%\,M_\oplus}$ ($24.6\times$ Ceres's mass), $\dot{m} = 7.34 \times 10^{10}\text{ kg/s}$, $P_{\text{jet}} = \mathbf{3.303 \times 10^{23}\text{ W}}$ ($0.086\%\,L_\odot$).
2. **Fatal Engineering & Safety Flaws When Attached to Earth:**
   - **Catastrophic Surface Waste Heat:** Operating $P_{\text{jet}} = 3.30 \times 10^{23}\text{ W}$ on an Earth-encircling ring at even $99\%$ efficiency generates $P_{\text{waste}} = 3.30 \times 10^{21}\text{ W}$ right inside Earth's near-space/atmospheric environment. The maximum allowable waste heat over Earth's entire surface ($5.10 \times 10^{14}\text{ m}^2 \times 5\text{ W/m}^2$) is only **$2.55 \times 10^{15}\text{ W}$**—so an Earth-mounted thruster exceeds the thermal limit by **$1,290,000\times$**!
   - **Structural Ring & Bearing Loads:** Transferring $F_{\text{req}} = 2.21 \times 10^{17}\text{ N}$ from an equatorial ring to Earth requires a hydrodynamic/magnetic bearing supporting $2.25 \times 10^{16}\text{ metric tons}$ of shear/normal load while Earth rotates underneath it at $465\text{ m/s}$, and requires a ring cross-sectional stress capacity orders of magnitude beyond carbon nanotubes.
   - **Verdict:** **NO-GO** when attached to Earth. (Moving the reaction-mass thruster $10^6\text{ km}$ off Earth turns it into the Detached Gravity Tug of Candidates A/F!)

### Candidate C — Pure Directed Energy & Photon Momentum
**Mechanism:** A Dyson-scale laser array shines a directed photon beam onto a reflector on Earth or a tug, transferring momentum via radiation pressure ($F = P / c$ for absorption/emission or $F = 2 P / c$ for specular reflection) with **zero reaction mass**.

1. **Governing Equations & Power Scale:**
   - Single reflection ($\mathcal{R} = 1$):
     $$P_{\text{beam, refl}} = \frac{F_{\text{req}} c}{2} = \mathbf{3.3072 \times 10^{25}\text{ W}} \quad (\mathbf{8.64\%}\text{ of total Solar Luminosity } L_\odot)$$
   - Single emission (photon rocket on tug):
     $$P_{\text{photon}} = F_{\text{req}} c = \mathbf{6.6143 \times 10^{25}\text{ W}} \quad (\mathbf{17.28\%}\text{ of } L_\odot)$$
   - Multi-bounce optical cavity ($N = 1,000$ bounces between a Dyson statite and an Earth/tug mirror): reduces circulating input power to $3.31 \times 10^{22}\text{ W}$, but requires maintaining phase/diffraction lock across $1\text{–}2.7\text{ AU}$ ($16\text{–}45\text{ minutes}$ round-trip light time!), where a moving mirror Doppler-shifts the beam and spoils cavity resonance.
2. **Fatal Thermal Flaw:**
   - Even if a mirror achieves $99.99\%$ reflectivity ($10^{-4}$ absorption), reflecting $3.31 \times 10^{25}\text{ W}$ absorbs **$3.31 \times 10^{21}\text{ W}$** of heat directly into the mirror film ($1.3 \times 10^6\times$ Earth's waste heat limit).
   - **Verdict:** **NO-GO** as a standalone momentum mechanism (though photon momentum of a $10^{25}\text{ W}$ power beam contributes a helpful $15.1\%$ thrust bonus in Candidates A and F).

### Candidate D — Solar Radiation Pressure & Large-Scale Mirrors
**Mechanism:** Giant solar sails or mirrors in heliocentric space reflect natural sunlight to generate tangential thrust $F_\theta(r) \propto 1/r^2$, coupled to Earth gravitationally or structurally.

1. **Governing $1/r^2$ Spiral Integration:**
   Because solar irradiance drops with distance as $S(r) = S_0 (r_1 / r)^2$, tangential thrust drops as $F_\theta(r) = F_{\theta,1\text{AU}} (r_1 / r)^2$. Substituting into the quasi-circular spiral rate $\frac{dr}{dt} = \frac{2 r^{3/2} F_\theta(r)}{M_\oplus \sqrt{GM_\odot}}$ and integrating from $r_1$ to $r_2$:
   $$\int_{r_1}^{r_2} r^{1/2}\,dr = \frac{2}{3}\left(r_2^{3/2} - r_1^{3/2}\right) = \frac{2 r_1^2 F_{\theta,1\text{AU}}}{M_\oplus \sqrt{GM_\odot}}\,T_{\max}$$
   $$\implies F_{\theta,1\text{AU}} = \mathbf{6.4569 \times 10^{17}\text{ N}} \quad (2.927\times \text{ higher than constant-thrust } F_{\theta,\text{avg}}\text{!})$$
2. **Required Mirror Area & Dyson-Shell Scale:**
   - For an ideal $45^\circ$ specular mirror where $F_\theta = \frac{S_0}{c} A_{\text{proj}}$:
     $$A_{\text{proj}} = \frac{F_{\theta,1\text{AU}} c}{S_0} = \mathbf{1.4223 \times 10^{23}\text{ m}^2} \quad (\mathbf{1.115 \times 10^9\times}\text{ Earth's cross-section!})$$
   - Equivalent circular mirror radius: **$R_{\text{mirror}} = 2.128 \times 10^8\text{ km} = \mathbf{1.422\text{ AU}}$** (larger than Earth's entire orbit!).
   - Fraction of the Sun's entire $4\pi$ steradian output intercepted: **$50.57\%$** (a literal half-complete Dyson sphere). Even at $1\text{ g/m}^2$ ultra-thin film, the mirror mass is $1.42 \times 10^{20}\text{ kg}$.
3. **Crucial Niche Where Mirrors DO Succeed (Habitability Concentrator at $2.7\text{ AU}$):**
   - While solar radiation pressure fails completely as a primary thruster, keeping Earth's insolation at $1,361\text{ W/m}^2$ out to $2.7\text{ AU}$ requires reflecting only $(2.7^2 - 1) = 6.29\times$ Earth's cross-section of sunlight:
     $$A_{\text{hab mirror}} = 6.29\,\pi R_\oplus^2 = \mathbf{8.02 \times 10^{14}\text{ m}^2} \implies R_{\text{hab mirror}} = \mathbf{15,978\text{ km}} \quad (2.51\,R_\oplus)$$
   - At $1\text{ g/m}^2$, this habitability mirror weighs only $8.0 \times 10^{11}\text{ kg}$ (a small 900-meter asteroid) and solves Earth's climate freezing problem at $2.7\text{ AU}$ with zero waste heat!
   - **Verdict:** **NO-GO** as standalone propulsion; **GO** as the climate/insolation subsystem in **Candidate F**.

### Candidate E — Multi-Body Gravity Assists & Solar-Wind / IMF Lorentz Arms
**Mechanism 1 (Steered Asteroid / Jupiter Gravity Assists):** Repeated close gravity-assist flybys of steered asteroids (or a Ceres shuttle looping between Jupiter and Earth) transfer orbital energy and angular momentum from Jupiter to Earth.  
**Mechanism 2 (Octo-Earth Lorentz Arms in External Magnetic Field):** Eight $12,000\text{ km}$ conducting arms push against an external magnetic field via $\mathbf{F} = I(\mathbf{L} \times \mathbf{B})$.

1. **Proof that Asteroid Gravity Assists Cannot Meet the 10,000-Year Limit:**
   - In a hyperbolic flyby of mass $m_{\text{ast}}$ past Earth at safe perigee $r_p \ge 2.5\,R_\oplus = 15,928\text{ km}$ (the fluid Roche limit, inside which an asteroid tidally shatters and rains debris onto Earth), the gravitational deflection angle $\delta$ satisfies $\sin(\delta / 2) = \left(1 + \frac{r_p v_\infty^2}{G M_\oplus}\right)^{-1}$.
   - The momentum transferred to Earth per pass is:
     $$\Delta p = 2 m_{\text{ast}} v_\infty \sin(\delta / 2) = \frac{2 m_{\text{ast}} v_\infty}{1 + r_p v_\infty^2 / (G M_\oplus)}$$
   - Maximizing with respect to $v_\infty$ yields the exact upper bound at $v_{\infty,\text{opt}} = \sqrt{G M_\oplus / r_p} = 5,002.6\text{ m/s}$ ($\delta = 60^\circ$):
     $$\left(\frac{\Delta p}{m_{\text{ast}}}\right)_{\max} = \sqrt{\frac{G M_\oplus}{r_p}} = \mathbf{5,002.6\text{ N}\cdot\text{s/kg}}$$
   - Therefore, imparting $J_{\text{spiral}} = 6.963 \times 10^{28}\text{ N}\cdot\text{s}$ requires a **minimum cumulative flyby mass** of:
     $$M_{\text{flyby, total}} = \frac{J_{\text{spiral}}}{5,002.6\text{ m/s}} = \mathbf{1.3918 \times 10^{25}\text{ kg}} = \mathbf{2.33\,M_\oplus} = \mathbf{14,806\times\text{ Ceres's mass!}}$$
   - Because the entire Main Asteroid Belt contains only $\sim 3 \times 10^{21}\text{ kg}$ ($0.02\%$ of the needed flyby mass), even using all of Ceres ($9.4 \times 10^{20}\text{ kg}$) in a reusable Earth–Jupiter shuttle loop requires **14,806 close passes**. Over $10,000\text{ years}$, that requires **one Ceres pass every $0.675\text{ years}$ ($247\text{ days}$)**—which is **dynamically impossible** because a Hohmann/gravity-assist round trip between Earth ($1\text{–}2.7\text{ AU}$) and Jupiter ($5.2\text{ AU}$) takes **$5.5\text{ to }8.5\text{ years}$ per orbit**! Furthermore, 14,806 Ceres passes at $2.5\,R_\oplus$ inside the Moon's orbit ($60\,R_\oplus$) would scatter and eject the Moon within the first dozen passes.
2. **Proof that Lorentz Arms Cannot Push Against Earth's Field or the Solar Wind:**
   - **Internal Field Fallacy:** Pushing conducting arms against Earth's own geomagnetic field ($\sim 3 \times 10^{-5}\text{ T}$) exerts an equal-and-opposite reaction force on Earth's outer core. By Newton's Third Law, internal forces sum to **zero net heliocentric force**.
   - **External Interplanetary Magnetic Field (IMF) / Solar Wind Limit:** The Sun's entire $4\pi$-steradian solar wind mass loss is $\dot{M}_{\text{sw}} \approx 2 \times 10^9\text{ kg/s}$ at $v_{\text{sw}} \approx 400\text{ km/s}$, carrying a **total omnidirectional momentum flux of only $\dot{M}_{\text{sw}} v_{\text{sw}} = 8.0 \times 10^{14}\text{ N}$**—which is **$276\times$ smaller than the required thrust $2.21 \times 10^{17}\text{ N}$ even if Earth captured 100% of the entire Sun's solar wind!** And in the $B_{\text{IMF}} \approx 5\text{ nT}$ field at $1\text{ AU}$, eight $12,000\text{ km}$ arms would need $I = 4.60 \times 10^{17}\text{ A}$, whose magnetic self-force would vaporize the arms instantly.
   - **Verdict:** **NO-GO** (violates momentum conservation and orbital period physics).

### Candidate F — Hybrid Architecture (Detached Variable-$d(t)$ Canted Gravity Tug + Beamed Power + L1 Solar Concentrator Mirror)
**Mechanism:** Combines the only physically viable components from Candidates A, C, and D into a closed, rulebook-compliant system:
1. **Momentum Transfer:** Detached Ceres-class gravity tug ($M_{\text{tug},0} = 9.4 \times 10^{20}\text{ kg}$) stationed ahead of Earth along $+\hat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}$ with separation $d(t) = d_0 \sqrt{M_{\text{tug}}(t) / M_{\text{tug},0}}$ shrinking from $1.303 \times 10^9\text{ m}$ to $5.017 \times 10^8\text{ m}$ so that $F_{\text{grav}}(t) = 2.2067 \times 10^{17}\text{ N}$ remains constant. Thrusters are canted by $\phi = 10^\circ$ to miss Earth by $14\text{–}36\,R_\oplus$ while consuming $7.50 \times 10^{20}\text{ kg} \le 8.0 \times 10^{20}\text{ kg}$ of reaction mass at $v_e = 0.3c$.
